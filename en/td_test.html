TD Test

Quality-assure your IBM Maximo

TD Test lets you test your IBM Maximo automatically – instead of someone clicking through the same checks by hand every time something changes. You describe once how a workflow is meant to behave, and from then on the test can be re-run as many times as you like: after an upgrade, after a new customisation, or ahead of every deployment.

The result is a quick answer to the question that matters most: does Maximo still work the way the business expects? If something has gone wrong, TD Test shows where in the flow it happened, what the error was and what the screen looked like at that moment. The tests do not touch your data – the database is left as it was, so the same test can be run any number of times without leaving a trace.

TD Test works just as well for older IBM Maximo 7.x as for the latest IBM Maximo Application Suite (MAS), and the same test can be run against both. That makes the tool especially useful during an upgrade: you can show, in black and white, that your customisations and processes work exactly as they did before in the new version.

Unlike ordinary testing tools, TD Test works directly against Maximo rather than through a browser. That makes the tests considerably faster, more stable and easier to maintain over time – you avoid rewriting them every time the interface changes.

CAPABILITIES

What can TD Test do?

"Testing Maximo with the help of Maximo."
TD Test works from inside Maximo, and so reaches the interface, the business logic and more.

Different kinds of test

With TD Test you can cover the parts of Maximo that matter most to the business – from what users click on to what happens in the background:

  • Maximo's web user interface
  • Maximo's business logic, for example the rules for how a work order may be changed
  • Integrations with other systems
  • Your own customisations of Maximo
  • Whole business processes, the way you actually work in Maximo – including flows where several users take turns, such as approval and handover, all within one and the same test

Reporting

Results are compiled automatically after every run and shown simply – in the web interface, or sent to where you already work, such as a Teams channel.

  • A result per test step, not just pass or fail for the test as a whole
  • On failure: the error message in plain text, together with the step that broke
  • A log extract from the moment of the failure, already pulled out for you
  • A screenshot of Maximo for each step, so you see what the test saw
  • History of earlier runs, so you can follow quality over time
  • Results can be sent automatically to a Teams channel

Flexibility

You decide when and how the tests are run.

  • Run on demand, for example during ongoing development work
  • Run a single test or a whole group of tests
  • Schedule runs so that they happen automatically
  • Start the tests from the browser – or let them form part of your existing automated pipelines (Jenkins, for example), if you have them. That is not a requirement.
  • Choose how much log detail is collected per test, without affecting your normal Maximo settings

BENEFITS

Why TD Test?

Fast

Because the tests do not have to detour through a browser, they are much faster. We have measured tests running up to 100 times faster with TD Test.

Stable

Ordinary testing tools drive a browser, which makes them sensitive to small things: a button that has moved, a page that loads slowly. TD Test talks directly to Maximo and checks the interface before it ever reaches the browser. The result is tests you can rely on, and that do not need rewriting every time something changes.

Cost-effective

TD Test is quick to get started with and needs no dedicated hardware of its own – it runs as a container in an environment you already have (Podman, Docker or Rancher, for example). Building tests for your particular business is fast too, so the benefit arrives early.

Easy to use

A test is written the way a user works in Maximo – step by step, using the same names that appear on the screen. Many steps also come ready to use, such as logging in or navigating to an application, so writing tests is quick and straightforward.

The tests are just as easy to follow in the web interface:

  • Clear reporting on the tests that have run
  • Easy to follow a test step by step
  • A precise answer on where a test goes wrong
  • Overview and follow-up of earlier runs
