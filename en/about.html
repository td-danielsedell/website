ABOUT US

We are Total Digital

The agility of a small company. The strength of a large one. That is Total Digital.

Technology meets domain knowledge. We combine leading platforms with deep domain knowledge and new technology to solve complex challenges – from new perspectives.

We like long-term relationships, high ambitions and people who know their field. Commitment and competence? We have a black belt in that.

HISTORY

How we got here

A lot has happened since we started in 2016. New colleagues, new areas of expertise and new assignments have taken us from a small specialist company to the Total Digital we are today. Here are some of the milestones along the way.

  1. 2016

    The start

    Total Digital is founded with a focus on IBM Maximo and asset management. A small specialist company takes its first steps.

  2. 2018

    We start to grow

    More and larger assignments grow the team. We strengthen our expertise and establish ourselves as specialists in IBM Maximo and asset management.

  3. 2021

    Maximo meets ArcGIS

    We begin to bring IBM Maximo and Esri ArcGIS together. Information about the condition of an asset is linked to its geographical position, opening up new ways of working with assets and infrastructure.

  4. 2025

    Remote sensing and AI

    We broaden our expertise with remote sensing and AI. That gives us new ways to detect change, analyse large areas and see the asset base in a wider context.

  5. 2026

    Locally present. Together as one team.

    Today we are in several places across Sweden, and on Åland too. We grow where the skills and the customers are, and work together as one team whatever the geography.
