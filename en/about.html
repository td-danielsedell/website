OUR JOURNEY

From Maximo to satellite data

A lot has happened since we started in 2018. New colleagues, new areas of expertise and new assignments have taken us from a small specialist company to the Total Digital we are today. Here are some of the milestones along the way.

  1. 2018

    We are founded

    Total Digital is founded by Linus, Jonas and Per, who between them have more than 60 years of experience in Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) and Maximo. A new consultancy is born.

  2. 2020

    Our first employees

    We hire for the first time. Over the year we double in number, the customer base grows and we take on our first managed-service clients.

  3. 2023

    TD Spatial is started

    At the end of 2023 we start a new business area, TD Spatial, focused on asset data combined with geodata. We begin to bring IBM Maximo and Esri ArcGIS together.

  4. 2024

    We grow fast

    More customers and more assignments call for more hands. Over the year we double our headcount and welcome new colleagues in both Maximo and geodata.

  5. 2025

    Remote sensing and AI

    We broaden our expertise with remote sensing and AI. That gives us new ways to detect change, analyse large areas and see the asset in a wider perspective.

  6. 2026

    Locally present, together as one team

    Today we are in several places across Sweden, and on Åland too. We grow where the skills and the customers are, and work together as one team regardless of location.

CERTIFICATIONS

Quality you can check

We are certified to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001

The certification covers the management system: the routines for how we take on assignments, run deliveries, handle deviations and improve over time.

For you as a customer it means our way of working is documented, followed up internally and audited regularly.

The certificates are issued by qValify.
