TD Linear

Understand your linear assets

TD Linear visualises and analyses data along linear assets such as railway, road, power lines and water and wastewater networks. Bringing Enterprise Asset Management (EAM), measurement and map data together in one shared view makes it easier to follow change over time, spot anomalies and understand how things relate along the whole asset.

Data from IBM Maximo can be shown alongside measurement data as stacked and linked interactive charts running the length of the linear asset. That answers the question that matters most, and answers it quickly: where did the anomalies occur, and what can they be connected to?

By analysing time series, TD Linear can bring out patterns and abnormal changes that are otherwise hard to spot. At the same time, point objects such as components and work orders can be set in relation to measurement data and other events along the route.

The result is a clear picture of how the asset is developing, and a better basis for analysis, maintenance and long-term decisions.
