SHOWCASES

We create value for our customers

Predictive railway safety with satellite data and AI

A satellite- and AI-based decision support system that forecasts rail temperature and the risk of track buckling 24–48 hours in advance. Combining multi-sensor satellite data, weather forecasts and infrastructure information makes proactive maintenance and safer railway operations possible.

>

High- and medium-resolution SAR processing for scalable monitoring

We process both high- and medium-resolution SAR data to produce detailed information about ground movement at local as well as regional scale. Applying data fusion techniques to medium-resolution SAR also allows consistent monitoring of large areas, supporting infrastructure analysis and decision-making.

>

AI-based leak detection in water infrastructure

We combine multi-sensor satellite data and AI to identify and locate leaks in water distribution networks by analysing subtle changes in the environment. By enabling continuous monitoring across large areas, the solution helps reduce water losses, lower operating costs and support a more sustainable water supply over the long term.

>

AI-based analysis of rail defects in image data

We develop methods for automatically detecting rail defects using deep learning and image data from railway inspections. By analysing visual patterns in track imagery, the system can identify defects at an early stage and support more efficient maintenance and higher operational reliability.
