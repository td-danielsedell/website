SELECTED PROJECTS

A selection of what we are working on right now

Predictive railway safety with satellite data and AI

A satellite- and AI-based decision support system that forecasts rail temperature and the risk of track buckling 24–48 hours in advance.

Synthetic Aperture Radar In(SAR) for scalable monitoring

We process high- and medium-resolution SAR data to map ground movement at both local and regional scale.

AI-based leak detection in water infrastructure

We combine multi-sensor satellite data and AI to identify and locate leaks in water distribution networks.

AI-based analysis of rail defects from imagery

We develop methods for automatically detecting rail defects using deep learning and image data from railway inspections.

PRODUCTS

Our experience, packaged for you

TD Asset Care

Asset management in practice

TD Asset Care makes it easy to manage and maintain physical assets. It combines proven Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) functionality with industry-specific features and can be deployed on-premises, hybrid or in the cloud.

TD Test

Quality-assure your IBM Maximo

TD Test automates testing of IBM Maximo and quality-assures your upgrades. It verifies that customisations and functions work as they should – from Maximo 7.x through to IBM Maximo Application Suite.

TD Linear

Understand your linear assets

TD Linear visualises and analyses data along linear assets. Bringing Enterprise Asset Management (EAM), measurement and map data together makes it easier to spot anomalies, understand how things relate and follow how the asset develops over time.
