Predictive railway safety with satellite data and AI

We develop AI-based forecasts of rail temperature and the risk of track buckling – for safer and more predictable railway operation in a warmer climate.

Higher temperatures and more frequent heatwaves increase the risk of track buckling in continuously welded rail, with disruption and delays as a result. By combining thermal satellite data, terrain and weather data and ground-based measurements, we can forecast temperature conditions along the track up to 48 hours in advance. That makes targeted, proactive action possible, and at the same time shows where the risk is greatest – and why.

The method is built for operational use, scales from individual high-risk stretches to entire railway networks, and supports maintenance prioritisation, traffic planning and long-term climate adaptation.

Partners: Region Stockholm, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, InfraNord
