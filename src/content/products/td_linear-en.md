You get a quick answer to the question that matters most: **where has the asset changed, and what can the change be connected to?** Bringing EAM, measurement and map data into one shared view shows exactly where a measured value deviates and which assets, work orders and earlier interventions sit in the same place.

Data from IBM Maximo, for instance, is shown alongside technical measurements in stacked and linked interactive charts. Several datasets and survey runs can be compared to show change over time, and requirement limits, warning levels and deviating segments can be marked up directly in the analysis.

With AI support, large volumes of data can be analysed faster. The result stays traceable to the underlying position and data, so every conclusion can be reviewed and verified.
