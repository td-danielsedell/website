TD Asset Care is a **proven solution for Enterprise Asset Management (EAM)**, built for organisations that want better control over their physical assets and maintenance.

It combines standard EAM functionality with industry-specific features and supports the full asset lifecycle – from planning and preventive maintenance through to corrective work, follow-up and analysis. **You get a solid foundation that can be adapted to your processes without starting from scratch.**

TD Asset Care can be deployed on-premises, hybrid or in the cloud, and integrates with your existing IT environment. The result is better **control**, **structured maintenance** and **greater value** from your assets.
