We develop InSAR-based solutions for monitoring ground motion across infrastructure and the built environment.

Using radar data from commercial SAR satellites, Sentinel-1 and NISAR, we can monitor changes across large areas regardless of weather conditions or time of day. This makes it possible to detect ground movement before the trend becomes visible in the field.

Depending on the project and ground conditions, we use Persistent Scatterer Interferometry (PSI) and Distributed Scatterer Interferometry (DSI) to provide reliable measurements over time.

The approach can be applied to individual structures or scaled across large geographical areas, with results integrated directly into existing systems. Applications include railways, roads, geotechnical monitoring, construction, dams and urban environments, supporting **long-term monitoring, risk management and informed decision-making.**
