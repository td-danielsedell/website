We combine AI, satellite data and operational data to detect **leaks and water losses** in large water distribution networks.

By analysing changes in ground conditions, vegetation and moisture levels over time, we can identify signs of leakage even before problems become visible in the field. Monitoring large areas helps locate potential leaks, identify risk areas and prioritise where action is needed.

The method supports continuous monitoring of the pipe network, helping **reduce water losses, improve maintenance planning**and **provide a better basis for decision-making.**
